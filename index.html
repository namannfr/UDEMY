Performance notes

Common mistakes in the first 90 days

01

Staying everyone's friend instead of earning respect as a leader. The team you used to sit with now needs you to make calls, not keep the peace.

02

Doing the work yourself instead of learning to delegate. What made you good as an individual contributor is the exact thing that will burn you out as a manager.

03

Avoiding the hard conversation until it becomes a bigger problem. Feedback delayed is trust delayed — and it compounds the wrong way.
